## Kant-Critique

Kant's Critique of Pure Reason and associated works. The idea that an AI can be modeled accordingly.

![kant](/images/Immanuel_Kant.jpg)

### Organization

As the effort is to create an agentic knowledge entity of this work, the textual rendering of the work (might be short considering the 1897 edition is 515 pages) serves as the basis for wrapping a machine intelligence around wherein the concepts of human reason can be comparmentalized by software.
